## Part I: THEORY — Bounded Intergenerational Curvature

### 1.1 Cain's Mark: The First a_min

Cain killed his brother. The highest curvature possible — nearly a singularity. Nearly infinite.

But God did not erase him. He gave him a mark.

> "וישם ה׳ לקין אות, לבלתי הכות אותו כל מוצאו"
> "And the LORD set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him"

The mark of Cain is **a_min** — the floor. The point below which one cannot fall. God said: you will not be erased. There is a limit to the fall. There is always a bounce.

**This is the first Big Bounce in history.** Not a Big Bang — a **bounce back**.

### 1.2 The Torah's Decay Law: 3-4 Generations

The Torah states:

> "פוקד עוון אבות על בנים, על שילשים ועל ריבעים"
> "Visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, unto the third and fourth generation"

This is not punishment. **This is physics.** When trauma occurs, the pain does not vanish in one generation. It propagates forward. But it is **bounded**. It weakens with each generation. 3-4 generations and —

```
Generation 0:  Trauma event — curvature κ ≈ κ_max (near-singularity)
Generation 1:  κ₁ = κ_max × decay — still high, survival mode
Generation 2:  κ₂ = κ₁ × decay — cooling, learning to live
Generation 3:  κ₃ = κ₂ × decay — straightening begins
Generation 4:  κ₄ ≈ 0 — bounce complete, new equilibrium

Bounded decay: κ(g) = κ₀ × r^g where r < 1, g = generation
After 3-4 generations: κ → 0 (not ∞, not stuck — bounded convergence)
```

### 1.3 The Holocaust as Near-Singularity

The Holocaust was the closest to a singularity that humanity has known. The curvature was nearly infinite. Survivors carried that curvature forward:

| Generation | Role | Curvature | Pattern |

| **3 — Great-grandchildren** | The straightening generation | κ₃ ≈ low | Curvature begins to flatten. What they feel — anxiety, difficulty, self-doubt — are **residues**, not defects |

**The anxiety is not a defect. It is the last oscillation of a bounce that started generations ago.**
